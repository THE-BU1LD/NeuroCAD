# Novelty audit

## Finding

No new neural architecture, CAD representation class, geometry algorithm, or state-of-the-art result is established. Typed ASTs, schema validation, CSG, OpenSCAD execution, unit normalization, and retrieval baselines all have substantial precedent.

## What is distinctive but not claimed as algorithmic novelty

- a fully traceable, fail-closed pipeline for a deliberately narrow language;
- separation of syntax/type/reference/constraint/static geometry checks from kernel execution and mesh topology checks;
- explicit preservation of a falsified historical parser claim while assigning new experiment IDs to repaired software;
- frozen per-example records, paired tests, uncertainty intervals, generated programs, kernel artifacts, failures, and figures from one command.

## Novelty decision

The manuscript is framed as a rigorous engineering evaluation and negative-evidence case study, not an ML breakthrough. Publication venues requiring a novel learned method or broad CAD generalization would be inappropriate without a new study. The strongest useful claim is reproducible correctness within a declared compiler contract.
