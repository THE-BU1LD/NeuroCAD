# Adversarial pre-execution review

**Disposition:** `METHOD READY / CLAIMS NARROW / CONFIRMATORY RESOURCE BLOCKED`

This review was performed after the version-1 study source was locked and
before confirmatory case materialization. It does not change the frozen
generator, endpoints, thresholds, or analysis.

## Strongest objections

1. **The primary behavior is partly tautological.** A stored content hash is
   expected to disagree after the hashed content changes. This is a controlled
   implementation-conformance result, not discovery of a new phenomenon.
2. **The authors control the synthetic cases.** Exact manifests and a
   single-attempt ledger limit outcome flexibility, but they do not create
   independent data, authorship, execution, or adjudication.
3. **The unbound arm lacks the tested mechanism by definition.** A paired
   full-versus-unbound freshness difference demonstrates the consequence of
   adding that mechanism in this harness; it is not a general superiority claim
   over other workflows or products.
4. **The three semantic mappings are narrow.** Cylinder diameter/length,
   isolated primitive volume, and isolated surface area do not cover Boolean
   CSG, assemblies, contacts, meshing, boundary-condition placement, or solver
   state.
5. **Provenance is not validity.** Exact hashes can faithfully preserve a bad
   model, unreliable property source, omitted load, or unsafe interpretation.
6. **Material and load cards are embedded declarations.** The report binds
   their content but exposes no external material-database or requirements-
   system freshness check. Only the selected geometry has a re-resolution API.
7. **Timing samples are conditional.** The harness retains elapsed time only
   for successful arm evaluations; rejected full-binding contradictions do not
   contribute timing values. Timing is therefore descriptive and cannot be
   treated as a balanced arm comparison.
8. **Request hashes include path text.** Request-content identity may change
   after path relocation even when the canonical geometry hash is unchanged.
   Cross-location reproduction should compare geometry and context content
   hashes, not expect the whole request hash to remain invariant.
9. **No physical or solver validation is present.** Unit tests and analytical
   formula checks do not establish fit, manufacturability, safety, or agreement
   with qualified FEA/CFD or experiment.

## Required manuscript controls

- Use “conformance,” “receipt,” and “bounded synthetic matrix,” not “validated
  physics,” “digital-thread breakthrough,” or “safe design.”
- Report exact numerators and denominators; label Wilson intervals descriptive.
- Explain that McNemar's value summarizes a predeclared mechanism contrast and
  is not the primary decision rule or a population effect.
- State the conditional timing sample counts and prohibit cross-system claims.
- Keep external independence, physical validation, and commercial-system
  comparisons explicitly absent.
- Preserve any negative, mixed, invalid, or blocked disposition verbatim.

## What would materially strengthen a successor

- Independent case authorship, blind execution, and adjudication.
- External CAD/PLM or CAE adapters with governed persistent identifiers.
- Re-resolvable material and requirements sources, signatures, and access
  control.
- Boolean part/assembly geometry plus independently verified reference values.
- Solver verification, physical experiments, uncertainty analysis, and
  qualified engineering review for any physical-use claim.
