# NeuroCAD verified-intent upgrade plan

Status: engineering implementation in progress on top of `0a4ce929c71b767e59822faf2a04546e324fe411`.
This extends [the existing architecture](NEUROCAD_EXPANSION_2026Q4.md), rather than replacing its IRs or reopening its frozen studies.
The execution contract and retained baseline are in `research/development/verified_intent_20261014/`.

NeuroCAD has no supported trained CAD checkpoint. Its supported compiler is deterministic, with optional language providers and exact-CAD/mesh backends. The old generated-grammar study is terminal mixed and does not measure a fair SOTA win or loss. Its causal typed-parser claim remains falsified. Universal superiority and perfection cannot be established by a plan or by passing a finite test suite.

The practical target is reliable, editable, requirement-verified engineering CAD. Broad text-to-CAD competitiveness is a separate measurement track. Until a human selects a different priority, engineering correctness comes first. Neither track can borrow success from the other.

## Diagnosis and upgrade order

The coverage, data and planning explanations below are hypotheses to test, not proven causes of a benchmark loss. Each proposed implementation needs a bounded development contract before execution. No row is a promise that failure is impossible.

| P0: output geometry predicates | `feature_presence` and `feature_count` currently inspect construction history. ## Current research changes the comparison plan

Recent work already uses prompt-derived geometric/topological tests to evaluate and guide generation. This means “verification-guided CAD” alone is not a novelty claim. NeuroCAD should test the narrower contribution of requirement preservation through editable revisions with auditable acceptance. [CADTests, May 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.07807).

Task difficulty must include advanced features and prompt style. Text2CAD-Bench spans four complexity/application levels and pairs geometric and procedural descriptions; success on basic primitives cannot stand in for that coverage. [Text2CAD-Bench, May 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.18430).

Clarification is also established prior work: ProCAD separates prompt clarification from coding and trains on clarification trajectories. Use it as a relevant comparison direction rather than claiming that asking questions is new. [ProCAD, revised June 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.03045).

Representation and dataset coverage deserve controlled ablations. CADFS reports a FeatureScript representation, 15 operations and 450,000 models, with component ablations. These are the authors' reported results, not evidence of compatibility, license suitability, reproducibility here or a NeuroCAD comparison. [CADFS, May 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.01925).

## Prospective experiment design — draft, not execution authorization

Freeze the primary task before choosing a model. For engineering utility, the primary endpoint should be complete must-requirement satisfaction after the requested edit, including preservation of unchanged requirements. Keep geometric accuracy, editability, unsafe acceptance, unnecessary clarification, latency and cost as separate endpoints. An invalid/timed-out/unsupported output is not silently dropped.

Use independent authors and adjudicators with source-family/author clusters. Determine sample size from a predeclared practically meaningful improvement and pilot variance, then freeze the confirmatory sample before any protected access. Predeclare the confidence level, multiple-comparison correction, cluster resampling scheme/seed, missing-output handling and stopping budget. The existing external compiler protocol retains its own fixed minimum and statistical requirements; this broader study needs a separately approved protocol. The new development comparator deliberately provides no inferential p-value.

The minimal causal matrix uses the same model revision, task set and inference budget: deterministic baseline; typed planner only; planner plus clarification; planner plus final-geometry verification; planner plus verification and bounded repair; full pipeline with edit-preservation checks. Hold representation/data fixed while isolating inference changes, then separately compare operation vocabulary and training-data changes. Report cross-seed variation and all attempted configurations. Do not select variants using protected outcomes.

A superiority claim requires a predeclared practical margin with a confidence interval above that margin on the selected task, no unacceptable increase in unsafe acceptance, and disclosed latency/cost/coverage tradeoffs. A win on one task does not establish dominance over every CAD system. If the effect is absent, mixed or negative, close that study honestly.

## Six scientific/product gates still apply

1. Freeze a new contract before outcomes; preserve v2 and all predecessor failures.
2. Independently derive or implement numerical and geometric references. Authorship by this implementation effort is disclosed even when the calculation is separately derived.
3. Execute complete installed CAD-to-analysis/revision workflows on the exact release candidate. Reuse stale-result invalidation checks; do not transfer older release evidence to new source.
4. For physical claims, supply apparatus, calibration, measured uncertainty, repetitions and a fixed applicability envelope; collect real measurements before assessing accuracy.
5. Have an executor outside implementation reproduce the frozen package and retain discrepancies. CI and AI review are not that executor.
6. Have intended users perform frozen tasks; retain outcomes and observed usability fixes.

No software change can manufacture the people, measurements, rights or independent decisions required by gates 4–6. Those are concrete open dependencies, not failures to be hidden.
