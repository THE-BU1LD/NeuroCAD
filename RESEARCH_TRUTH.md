# Research truth

- NeuroCAD is a deterministic bounded compiler, not an ML model.
- Release candidates are engineering-verified only after tests, static checks, and kernel-backed cases pass on the exact revision.
- A 24-case audit-authored adversarial development challenge passed completely;
  its post-inspection authorship prevents treating it as independent confirmation.
- A unit-aware dimensions-only baseline improves over raw number copying but still
  cannot explain feature-aware hole, slot, or enclosure behavior.
- The historical FULL run reports perfect controlled results but is **development evidence**: its manifest is not source-bound and 181/240 kernel records were resumed.
- `NC-REPRO-508EC40` is the current source-bound controlled run: clean commit `508ec404dd520ae16f2b4d3cf211d5b1fa46b800`, 1,212 hashed artifacts, 1,000/1,000 IR programs, 240/240 compiler cases, and 240/240 freshly compiled kernel cases with zero reuse.
- The generated benchmark tests conformance to the compiler's own declared grammar; it is not evidence of open-world natural-language generalization.
- The historical typed-parser causal claim is falsified. No later compiler result rehabilitates it.
- No trained checkpoint, real STEP/BREP path, physical validation, safety proof, public release receipt, or independent replication exists.
- Current conference-readiness verdict: **EVIDENCE_PARTIAL**. Local reproducibility now passes; independent data, a strong compatible baseline, external replication, citation audit, and final-PDF gates remain open.
