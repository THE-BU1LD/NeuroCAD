# Human/practical evaluation protocol — not executed

No human usability claim is made in this release. NC-EXP-005 measures programmatic parameter editability only.

A future study would recruit qualified CAD users, pre-register tasks and exclusions, and compare canonical NeuroCAD IR with raw OpenSCAD under a within-subject counterbalanced design. Tasks would change a named dimension, feature count, and Boolean relationship. Primary outcome: valid target geometry after one edit. Secondary outcomes: completion time, repair count, and blinded expert correctness. Evaluators would receive identical task instructions and no system labels. Kernel validation would be automated; subjective ratings would be secondary. Power analysis, consent, privacy, and institutional review requirements must be settled before recruitment.

Until that study runs, phrases such as “easy to use,” “human-editable,” or “faster for designers” are unsupported. The allowed phrase is “named parameters survived automated edit, validation, round trip, and export in 200/200 controlled cases.”
