# Research

NeuroCAD asks whether a deterministic, typed, fail-closed compiler can translate
a declared dimensioned-English subset into semantically exact executable CAD
programs more reliably than simple controls. It is not a learned-model study.

- Current hypotheses: `../research/HYPOTHESES.md`
- Mathematical formulation: `../research/MATHEMATICAL_SPEC.md`
- Evidence: `../RESEARCH_TRUTH.md` and `../EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md`
- Novelty: `../research/NOVELTY_AUDIT.md`
- Final audit: `../audit/FINAL_AUDIT.md`

Current verdict: **EVIDENCE_PARTIAL**. Software behavior and development runs are
verified; independent confirmatory evidence is absent.
